# 拾光屿 v1.0.3 / Shiguang Isle v1.0.3

验收结果见 [验证记录 / Validation records](https://github.com/Undefeated-man/shiguang-isle/blob/main/docs/validation.md)。

## 简体中文

图库的筛选、多选与排序栏在照片滚动时保持在顶部。无需回到页面开头，即可调整日期、色系、主题与标签，改变排序或操作已选照片。

工具栏随窗口大小和字号自适应，照片定位为实际工具栏高度预留空间；日期时间轴仍用于快速定位当前筛选结果。打开、关闭日历或菜单以及确认选项后，返回焦点保持当前滚动位置，避免图库突然回到顶部。

原片、收藏、标签与主题保持不变。浏览使用可清理的轻量预览缓存，不创建完整原片副本。

安装包提供 Apple Silicon / Intel macOS 的 DMG 与 ZIP，以及 Linux x64 的 AppImage 与 deb。下载后可用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对完整性。macOS 使用 ad-hoc 签名，未进行 Apple 公证。

顶部固定交互已在原生 Electron 的常规与小窗口合成图库中分别通过 40 项断言，覆盖 150% 字号、深滚动、跨分页日期定位、筛选和多选操作，以及日历 / 菜单交互后的滚动位置保持。本地 macOS arm64 最终安装包通过 189 项完整烟雾测试和 24 项独立图库测试，共 213 项；实际复制、原片哈希、设置与可恢复 SD 同步核对通过，签名、DMG 与 1399 个打包文件的脱敏检查通过。三平台 CI 的验收结果待补充。

## English

The gallery's filtering, selection, and sorting toolbar now stays at the top while photographs scroll. Adjust dates, colors, themes, and tags, change the order, or act on selected photographs without returning to the beginning.

The toolbar adapts to the window and font size. Photo navigation reserves its actual height, while the date timeline continues to jump through the current filtered results. Returning focus after opening or closing the calendar or a menu, or confirming an option, preserves the current scroll position instead of jumping to the top.

Originals, favorites, tags, and themes remain unchanged. Browsing uses removable lightweight previews rather than full original copies.

Packages include DMG and ZIP for Apple Silicon and Intel macOS, plus AppImage and deb for Linux x64. Verify downloads using `SHA256SUMS.txt`. macOS packages use ad-hoc signatures and are not Apple-notarized.

The sticky controls passed 40 assertions in both regular and compact native Electron synthetic libraries, covering 150% font size, deep scrolling, cross-page date navigation, filtering, selection, and preserved scroll position after calendar or menu interactions. The final local macOS arm64 package passed 189 smoke assertions and 24 independent gallery assertions, totaling 213. Production copies, original hashes, persisted settings, and recoverable SD synchronization were verified, along with its signature, DMG, and privacy checks of 1,399 packaged files. Three-platform CI results will be added after testing.
