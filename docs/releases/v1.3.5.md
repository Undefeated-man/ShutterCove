# Shutter Cove v1.3.5 · Color, motion, and 时光杂货店

A desktop photo organizer and creative studio for DSLR and mirrorless photographers shooting JPEG / Canon CR3, with paired RAW browsing, collage posters, GIFs, and memes.

- **Custom collage colors.** Choose background and text colors with a color picker or enter a 3 / 6-digit HEX value. Titles, subtitles, and imprints share your text color; the wooden board keeps its grain with your background tint. Switching templates or languages preserves your choices, and Restore template colors returns to the current template defaults. Share preview and save still use the same encoded JPEG bytes.
- **A gallery with gentle motion.** Newly visible photographs enter in a bounded, staggered spring cascade. Buttons compress to 0.96 with inset depth and settle after a subtle rebound. Photographs expand continuously from their cards into the detail viewer, with a matching return transition. These brief, in-memory effects respect the system's Reduce motion preference and release their decorative layers after use.
- **A calmer glass interface.** Warm ivory and sage surfaces, clearer visual hierarchy, refined spacing, and readable controls adapt to small windows and larger font settings. Filtering, sorting, and selection remain available in the sticky toolbar.
- **A new Chinese name.** The Chinese interface is now **时光杂货店**, formerly 拾光屿. The English brand remains **Shutter Cove**. The Chinese wordmark stays on one line when space permits and breaks only as 时光 / 杂货店 in a narrow sidebar. Your library, favorites, tags, settings, and language choice continue to use the same application identity and data location.

The native desktop app remains available for macOS and Linux. Download the matching DMG for Apple Silicon or Intel, or an AppImage / deb for Linux x64, from [official Releases](https://github.com/Undefeated-man/ShutterCove/releases). Verify downloads with the same-version `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Compatibility filenames retain `Shiguang-Isle-`, and Mac packages contain `Shiguang Isle.app`; these are the same app. Existing Mac installations and writable AppImages update in place from Settings. Mac packages use ad-hoc signing and are not notarized; see the [Mac installation guide](https://github.com/Undefeated-man/ShutterCove/blob/main/docs/mac-install.md).

Fresh installations choose English or Chinese before the usage guide; existing profiles retain their choice. Japanese and Korean guides are documentation translations, while the current app supports English and Simplified Chinese.

## 中文

- **拼图背景、文字自由配色。** 支持取色器与 3 / 6 位 HEX 输入，标题、副标题和页脚统一文字颜色，木质展示板保留木纹并随背景染色。切换模板、界面语言时保留自定义颜色，也可一键恢复当前模板默认色。分享预览与保存仍为同一份 JPEG 成品字节。
- **让相册有轻微呼吸感。** 新出现的照片交错弹性上滑入场，按钮按下缩至 0.96、增加内阴影，释放轻微回弹。照片卡片与大图详情连续缩放转场，关闭时回到原卡片。动画遵循系统“减少动态效果”，不会生成中间文件或挡住缩放、拖动、导航操作。
- **更清楚的毛玻璃层次。** 暖白与鼠尾草绿统一界面、改善留白和控件对比度，适配紧凑窗口与大字号。筛选、多选、排序仍固定在顶部。
- **中文名更新为「时光杂货店」。** 原名「拾光屿」，英文品牌继续为 Shutter Cove。字标在空间足够时完整显示，需要换行时固定为「时光 / 杂货店」，兼顾窄窗口与大字号。应用标识和数据位置保留，收藏、标签、照片索引、设置与语言选择延续使用。

本次仍为 macOS / Linux 桌面 App。安装包兼容名称仍为 `Shiguang-Isle-`，Mac 包内为 `Shiguang Isle.app`，均是同一应用。可从设置原位更新；下载后请核对同版本校验表。首次 Mac 安装可能需要按 [Mac 安装说明](https://github.com/Undefeated-man/ShutterCove/blob/main/docs/mac-install.md) 允许打开。当前界面支持中英两种语言，首次安装先选择语言，已有中文用户继续使用中文。
